# The files where this game's own patches (scripts/dependency-patches) or the commits its submodules pin change the
# same lines as N64Recomp-browser's web support: <project> <path in the project>. tools/build_web.sh leaves each out of
# N64Recomp-browser's patch for its project and applies web/patches/<project>.patch in its place, the web's change to
# these files merged by hand on top of the game's. The web's later changes to them are merged in by apply.cmake --since
# from the commit below; one that meets the game's own lines stops the build, for another merge by hand and this
# commit moved.
#
# rt64 (after web/patches/rt64-upstream.patch): the game's present queue keeps its pause screen's roll transition
# beside the web's pacing; its external pause (the game's own pause menu) sits beside the web's late RAM copies in
# updateScreen(); its full-height targets for a pair spanning most of the screen come after the web's viewport height,
# and its Framebuffer objects take the height from between the two; its present fill modes (pillarbox, crop, stretch)
# give the scale the web rounds to whole pixels.
# N64ModernRuntime: the game's VI thread queues its boot's dummy workload and the screen update under the message
# mutex, before the VI's state moves on; with ULTRAMODERN_SCREEN_UPDATE_AFTER_VI (on in a page) the update comes after it,
# as the web's does.
# RecompFrontend (the game's own copy, in its tree): the game's rumble goes through its send_controller_rumble, the
# page's through web_rumble. Its get_n64_input reads a controller player's keyboard only if asked (include_keyboard),
# and the web's rule of which players read the keyboard (player_reads_profile) comes before. Its controls page keeps its
# own profile index (set_current_profile_index) after the web's switch out of single-player mode, and shows the keyboard
# toggle and keys by the web's controller_player_has_keyboard.
